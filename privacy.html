Privacy Policy

Z2 Tracker

Last updated: October 2026

What Z2 Tracker reads

With your permission, Z2 Tracker reads your heart rate, workouts and date of birth from Apple Health. It does not write anything to Apple Health.

How it is used

This data is used only to calculate your heart rate zones and show your Zone 2 training time inside the app. Your age is used to estimate your maximum heart rate.

Where it is stored

All processing happens on your iPhone. Z2 Tracker has no servers and does not upload, sell or share your health data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Your settings

Preferences such as your Zone 2 goal and custom zones are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Managing access

You can turn Health access on or off at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Z2 Tracker.

Not medical advice

Z2 Tracker is a fitness tool, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program.
