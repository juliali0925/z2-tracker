Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see any data?

Z2 Tracker uses heart rate recorded during workouts, usually from an Apple Watch. Record a workout (for example with the Workout app), then open Z2 Tracker and tap refresh.

Health access is turned off. How do I turn it back on?

Open the iPhone Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Z2 Tracker, and turn on Heart Rate, Workouts and Date of Birth.

How are my zones calculated?

By default, your maximum heart rate is estimated as 220 minus your age, and Zone 2 is 60–70% of that. If you know your own numbers, turn on Custom Zones in Settings.

Does Z2 Tracker upload my health data?

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
